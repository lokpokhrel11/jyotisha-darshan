# Chapter 6.4 source verification
Primary: 117-page Jyotisha study notes, October 2026, PDF pp.56–59, section 6.4. Standard 8 Yoginis: Mangala Moon 1, Pingala Sun 2, Dhanya Jupiter 3, Bhramari Mars 4, Bhadrika Mercury 5, Ulka Saturn 6, Siddha Venus 7, Sankata Rahu 8; 36 years. First yogini index: (nakshatra ordinal + 3) modulo 8, remainder 0 means Sankata. Remaining duration = (bhavog-bhayat)/bhavog × yogini years. Rohini ordinal 4 → Siddha; example fraction ~0.7379 × 7 = 5.17 years. Alternative handwritten 9-Yogini variant divides Sankata 8 into Sankata 4 and Vikata 4; its usage is unresolved and excluded from this calculator. Educational 360-day conversion only; no calendar dates or predictions.
