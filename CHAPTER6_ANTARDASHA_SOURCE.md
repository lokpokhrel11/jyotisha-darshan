# Chapter 6.2 source mapping
Original 117-page study notes (October 2026), Section 6.2, PDF pp.55–56: antardasha sequence begins with the mahadasha lord and proceeds through all nine lords. Duration = major years × sub-lord years / 120. Examples Moon–Moon = 10 months, Moon–Mars = 7 months, Moon–Rahu = 18 months. Conversion to months = major years × sub-lord years / 10.
Implementation uses 360-day educational year, so each duration in days is exactly major years × sub-lord years × 3. Does not derive actual birth antardasha or dates.
