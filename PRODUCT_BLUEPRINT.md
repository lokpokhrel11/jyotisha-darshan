# Jyotisha Darshan · Product Blueprint v0.1

**Status:** Free 12–18-month learning and validation pilot, November 2026.

## Mission and scope
One independent brand with three prospective lines: (1) Jyotisha Pathshala education, (2) Jyotiṣa Consultations, and (3) a curated Expert Network. Independent from Sattva Intelligence. No active commercial services or implied endorsements.

## Source of curriculum
User-designated 117-page compilation, *ज्योतिष शास्त्र: अध्ययन नोट (गुरुजीका कक्षामा आधारित)* (October 4, 2026). The notes distinguish [गुरुजी], [शास्त्रीय], and [पुष्टि गर्नुपर्ने]. Preserve those distinctions; obtain permission for reproducing original notes or substantial extracts. Curriculum order: pañcāṅga → chart structure → grahas → calculations → interpretive grammar → thematic analysis. Interpretive sequence: bhāva → rāśi → graha → graha condition → relationships/aspects → traditional phala.

## Initial free platform
GitHub Pages, static HTML/CSS/JS, PWA, no accounts, no analytics tracking by default, no client data. Interactive lessons and local-only practice. The consultations page is informational only. No public practitioner roster without consent.

## Future revenue streams (not active)
- Free entry-level education; paid advanced courses and instructor-led cohorts.
- Fixed-fee, clearly scoped consultations and human-reviewed reports.
- Curated expert marketplace with agreed percentage of fees retained by the platform.
- Optional professional calculation and reporting tools.

## Marketplace design for later
Entities: practitioner, verified qualifications/experience, service, schedule, booking, client consent, transaction, refund, commission, payout, dispute. Practitioners must opt in and sign agreements. Separate roles: owner/admin, educator, practitioner, learner, client. Each practitioner accesses only authorized client records. Platform commissions calculated against a contractually defined fee base; explicit policies for refunds, tax, payment processing, chargebacks, and international payouts.

## Milestones
**0–3 months:** launch static website, introductory Pathshala lessons, sign/house chart practice, source QA.
**3–6 months:** build additional chapter exercises and testable calculation modules, practitioner workflow mockups using fictional data.
**6–12 months:** evaluate training and content reliability, draft practitioner onboarding and service standards, study market demand.
**12–18 months:** assess launch readiness, privacy/legal/compliance requirements, potential secure backend and custom domain.

## Quality gates
1. Verified content and clear provenance.
2. Unit-tested astronomical/calendar mathematics before public calculator claims.
3. Human-reviewed interpretation; no deterministic claims of verified predictive accuracy.
4. No health, legal, or financial advice substitutes.
5. Consent, confidentiality, security and dispute procedures before real consultations.
6. No promise of credentials or partnerships absent formal agreement.
